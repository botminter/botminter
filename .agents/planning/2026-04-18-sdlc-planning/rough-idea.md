# Rough Idea: Redesign SDLC Planning Phase

Redesign the planning phase of the BotMinter Agentic SDLC. This includes:

1. **What planning produces** — define the artifacts (specs, ADRs, design docs, implementation plans) that come out of the planning phase, where they live, and what format they follow.

2. **Adapt PDD for the agentic team context** — take the upstream PDD methodology and tweak it for the BotMinter profile: architect role ownership, Ralph orchestration, multi-member handoff, and how planning interacts with the board/statuses.

3. **Wire planning outputs into downstream SDLC** — ensure implementation has structured inputs to work from. Planning artifacts should feed directly into the statuses and workflows that follow (e.g., stories created from the plan, acceptance criteria from the design, etc.).

The goal is to make planning a first-class, artifact-producing phase of the SDLC rather than an informal step where an epic goes to "planning" status and the architect does... something undefined.
